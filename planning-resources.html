Collect Passport Stamps as You Explore Featured Parks

To get started, download the DPARD 150 app from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store.

As you explore the featured parks in this passport, scan the QR code at each park entrance to check in and confirm your visit. Each scan unlocks a digital passport stamp in the app, helping you track your journey while discovering 150 years of Dallas Park and Recreation history.

Want a physical keepsake? Collect FREE stamps for your passport booklet at any of the stamp stations highlighted on our map. Visit, scan, stamp, and explore as you celebrate DPARD’s 150-year legacy across Dallas!

Explore Dallas’s Historic Parks Through Interactive Maps

Experience the rich legacy of DPARD and Dallas by exploring some of its most iconic parks on our interactive maps. Each featured destination offers a unique glimpse into the people, events, and milestones that have shaped Dallas over the past 150 years.

Wander scenic trails, relax in historic green spaces, and uncover fascinating stories as you journey through our city’s parks. We invite you to discover the history, beauty, and lasting impact of the parks that continue to connect and inspire generations of Dallas residents.
