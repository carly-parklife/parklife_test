A Neighborhood Park with Deep Roots

Established in 1974, Campbell Green Park is a 17.5-acre community park that has served as a gathering place for generations of North Dallas residents. Named in honor of the historic Campbell family, who played a significant role in the development of the surrounding area, the park reflects the neighborhood’s growth and evolution while continuing to provide a welcoming space for recreation, connection, and community life.

What began as a modest neighborhood green space has grown into a well-loved destination offering a variety of amenities for visitors of all ages. Over the years, Campbell Green Park has expanded to include athletic facilities, outdoor recreation areas, and community programming opportunities that support active lifestyles and neighborhood engagement.

Today, the park remains a vital part of the community, offering residents a place to exercise, gather with friends and family, and enjoy the outdoors. Its combination of recreational amenities and community-focused spaces has helped make Campbell Green a lasting neighborhood staple.

Recreation and Outdoor Amenities

Campbell Green Park provides a diverse range of amenities designed to encourage both active recreation and relaxation. The park features tennis and pickleball courts, sand volleyball courts, walking trails, soccer fields, a playground, and open green spaces that accommodate a variety of activities throughout the year.

One of the park’s most popular features is its sprayground, providing a fun and accessible way for children and families to cool off during warmer months. The park also includes a bark park, giving four-legged visitors a dedicated area to play and socialize. Together, these amenities create a destination that serves residents of all ages and interests.

The Campbell Green Recreation Center serves as a central hub for community activities. The facility offers a fitness gym, gymnasium, and a variety of programs designed to promote wellness, education, and social connection. From youth activities and sports programs to fitness opportunities and community events, the recreation center has played an important role in supporting the needs of nearby residents.

A New Chapter: Recreation Center Renovation and Expansion

In 2026, Campbell Green Park entered an exciting new era with the beginning of a major renovation and expansion project at the Campbell Green Recreation Center. Funded through the City of Dallas’ voter-approved 2024 Bond program, the project represents a significant investment in the future of the park and the surrounding community.

The recreation center closed on May 31, 2026, to allow construction crews to complete a comprehensive remodel aimed at creating a larger, more modern facility. The upgraded center is designed to enhance community programming, improve accessibility, and provide expanded spaces for recreation and neighborhood services. The renovated facility is expected to reopen in fall 2027, bringing new opportunities for residents to connect, learn, and participate in community activities.

As part of the transformation, the city is also incorporating new public art into the expanded recreation center. With an investment of up to $136,000, the artwork will introduce bright, playful elements inspired by animals, nature, and rainbows. The project reflects Dallas’ commitment to integrating creativity and community identity into public spaces while making recreation facilities more welcoming and engaging.

A Lasting Legacy for Future Generations

For more than five decades, Campbell Green Park has provided a place where neighbors can gather, children can play, and families can enjoy the outdoors. Its continued improvements demonstrate the importance of investing in community spaces that serve changing generations while preserving the character and history of local neighborhoods.

With its expanded recreation center, enhanced amenities, and renewed focus on community connection, Campbell Green Park is poised to continue its legacy as one of North Dallas’ treasured neighborhood parks. The ongoing improvements ensure that the park will remain a vibrant destination where residents can celebrate recreation, creativity, and community for years to come.
