150 Years of Setting the Standard for Parks and Recreation in America

For 150 years, the Dallas Park and Recreation Department (DPARD) has been creating spaces, experiences, and opportunities that bring people together. From beloved parks and recreation centers to historic landmarks and community programs, DPARD’s legacy is built on a commitment to enriching lives, strengthening neighborhoods, and connecting generations.

Explore the People, Places, and Stories of DPARD

The DPARD 150 celebration honors this incredible milestone by inviting the community to explore the people, places, and stories that have shaped Dallas.

Through interactive digital experiences, featured locations, historic figures, audio tours, commemorative merchandise, and community storytelling, residents and visitors can discover the history behind the parks and programs that make Dallas unique.

Celebrating Our History & Building Our Future

This anniversary is more than a look back in time. It’s a celebration of what we have accomplished together and a vision for the future. DPARD continues to set the standard for urban parks and recreation by embracing progress, innovation, sustainability, accessibility, and equity.

Join us as we celebrate 150 years of memories made, communities strengthened, and a future built on creating even greater experiences for all.
