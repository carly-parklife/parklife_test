Reconnecting Downtown Dallas

Opened in 2012, Klyde Warren Park transformed the heart of Downtown Dallas by creating a vibrant 5.4-acre public park above the Woodall Rodgers Freeway. Stretching across three city blocks between Pearl Street and St. Paul Street, the park physically reconnects neighborhoods that had long been separated by the below-grade freeway. Today, it serves as a lively gathering place where residents, workers, and visitors come together to relax, attend events, dine, exercise, and enjoy one of Dallas’ most celebrated public spaces.

The park is named for Klyde Warren, the young son of businessman and philanthropist Kelcy Warren, whose $10 million donation helped make the project possible. More than simply creating new green space, Klyde Warren Park demonstrated how innovative urban design could transform existing infrastructure into a destination that enhances both community life and the city’s overall connectivity.

From Vision to Reality

The idea of building a park over the Woodall Rodgers Freeway dates back to the 1960s, when Dallas Mayor J. Erik Jonsson envisioned the freeway below street level, making future development above it possible. Decades later, the concept gained renewed momentum when civic leaders, developers, and community organizations recognized the opportunity to reconnect Downtown Dallas and the Arts District.

In 2004, The Real Estate Council provided funding for feasibility studies, while civic leaders including John Zogg, Jody Grant, and Linda Owen helped establish the Woodall Rodgers Park Foundation, the nonprofit organization that guided the project from concept to completion. Construction on the deck began in 2009, and after years of collaboration between public agencies, private donors, engineers, architects, and community advocates, Klyde Warren Park officially opened in October 2012.

The project quickly became a national example of successful public-private partnership, demonstrating how civic investment and community vision could reshape an urban landscape while creating lasting public value.

Innovative Design Above a Freeway

Designed by award-winning landscape architect Jim Burnett and his firm, The Office of James Burnett, Klyde Warren Park was carefully planned to create a series of inviting outdoor spaces that encourage exploration. Visitors move through distinct “rooms” within the park, each offering its own atmosphere and activities while remaining connected by beautifully landscaped walkways.

The park features children’s play areas, reading spaces, interactive fountains, lawns, dog parks, game areas, shaded seating, and a central performance pavilion with an adjoining restaurant. Throughout the year, these spaces host concerts, fitness classes, cultural festivals, outdoor movies, food trucks, and countless community events, making the park one of Dallas’ busiest public destinations.

Beneath the beautiful landscaping lies an impressive engineering achievement. The park deck is supported by more than 300 concrete beams, allowing the freeway to continue operating below while maintaining street-level access above. Specially designed trenches between the beams serve as planter boxes that support the growth of hundreds of trees and plants. Lightweight geofoam and engineered soils reduce the deck’s overall weight while providing the depth needed for healthy landscaping.

The result is a thriving urban park that feels natural despite being constructed above one of Dallas’ busiest transportation corridors. Sustainable landscaping includes 247 trees and dozens of native and adapted plant species, creating a lush green oasis in the center of the city.

A Landmark for Community and Culture

Since opening, Klyde Warren Park has become one of Dallas’ most recognizable public spaces and an important destination for recreation, arts, and community life. Its central location connects the Dallas Arts District, Uptown, Downtown, and nearby neighborhoods, making it easy for pedestrians to move between some of the city’s most popular cultural attractions.

The park’s flexible design supports everything from quiet afternoons reading under the trees to large public celebrations that draw thousands of visitors. Families gather at the playground, professionals enjoy lunch outdoors, fitness enthusiasts participate in group exercise classes, and visitors experience live performances on the park’s central stage. Throughout every season, the park serves as a welcoming space where people of all ages and backgrounds can come together.

The creation of Klyde Warren Park also inspired cities across the country to consider similar deck park projects that reconnect neighborhoods divided by highways. Its innovative combination of engineering, landscape architecture, and community-focused programming has earned widespread recognition as a model for urban revitalization.

Today, Klyde Warren Park stands as one of Dallas’ signature public spaces—a place where thoughtful design transformed unused infrastructure into a vibrant civic landmark. By bridging neighborhoods, expanding access to green space, and creating opportunities for recreation, culture, and connection, the park continues to demonstrate the lasting impact that visionary planning can have on a city’s quality of life.
