A Community Hub in Southeast Dallas

Established in 1984, Kleberg Park is a 47.2-acre community park located in southeast Dallas and serves as an important gathering place for the surrounding Kleberg-Rylie neighborhood. Officially known as the Kleberg-Rylie Recreation Center, the park is located at 1515 Edd Road and provides residents with recreational opportunities, educational programs, and community services. The park’s story is closely tied to the history of Kleberg, a once-independent rural community that became part of Dallas in the late 1970s.

The area known today as Kleberg has roots dating back to the 1840s, when it developed as a stagecoach stop and farming community. The land was originally granted to Robert Kleberg in 1845, and over time the settlement grew into a close-knit rural town. In 1956, Kleberg officially incorporated as its own city and became known for its unusual distinction as the largest town in Texas that did not collect property taxes. However, rising costs associated with maintaining utilities and infrastructure eventually led residents to consider annexation. On April 3, 1978, Kleberg residents voted to merge with the City of Dallas, becoming part of the growing metropolitan area.

Establishing a Neighborhood Recreation Center

Following Kleberg’s annexation, the Dallas Park and Recreation Department worked to expand public amenities and services in the area. In 1984, the department purchased approximately 20 acres of land from the Chamco Corporation to create what would become Kleberg-Rylie Park and Recreation Center. The site was selected to provide recreational opportunities and a central gathering space for the growing southeast Dallas community.

The recreation center was completed in 1989 and later renovated in 1998 to better serve residents. The park was named after the neighborhood it serves, preserving the historic Kleberg identity while connecting the area to the broader Dallas park system. The original site also had connections to community services, as the land was once planned for use as the location of the Kleberg-Rylie Branch Library.

Over the years, Kleberg Park has grown into a versatile community destination. Its facilities support youth programs, athletic activities, fitness opportunities, and neighborhood events that bring residents together.

Recreation, Wellness, and Community Programming

Today, Kleberg Park offers a wide range of amenities designed for recreation and community engagement. The park includes two baseball fields, two tennis courts, a gymnasium, a fitness center, meeting rooms, and a kitchen available for community use. Outdoor features include a public trail and a pond surrounded by mature trees, providing a peaceful setting within the neighborhood.

The Kleberg-Rylie Recreation Center serves as the heart of the park, offering programming for residents of all ages. From sports leagues and fitness activities to community gatherings and educational programs, the facility continues the Dallas Park and Recreation Department’s mission of providing accessible spaces where people can connect, learn, and stay active.

The park’s location also reflects its role as a neighborhood resource. It is surrounded by important community facilities, including the Kleberg-Rylie Branch Library and a Dallas Independent School District bus service depot to the north and east, with residential neighborhoods located across Edd Road to the south. Together, these nearby resources create a network of services that support local families.

A Place of Recognition and Community Pride

Kleberg Park gained national attention in 2012 when former First Lady Michelle Obama visited the recreation center as part of her initiative to encourage healthier food choices for children. During the visit, she joined celebrity chefs from the Bravo television series Top Chef and members of the Dallas Cowboys to promote healthier school lunches and discuss the importance of nutrition and wellness. The event highlighted the recreation center’s role as a place where national conversations about health and community well-being could take place.

More than four decades after its establishment, Kleberg Park remains a valued neighborhood asset. From its historic roots as an independent Texas town to its current role as a Dallas community center, the park represents the enduring connection between local history, recreation, and public service. Through its facilities, programs, and welcoming spaces, Kleberg Park continues to bring generations of residents together.
