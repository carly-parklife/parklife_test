A Destination for Recreation, Art, and Community

Bachman Lake Park is a 205-acre urban oasis that has been bringing people together for generations. The park grew around a reservoir constructed in 1903 and was formalized as a public park by the 1930s. Situated just north of Dallas Love Field Airport, the park offers a unique combination of natural beauty, recreation, public art, and community amenities.

Centered around Bachman Lake, the park provides visitors with opportunities to enjoy the outdoors, stay active, and experience the energy of one of Dallas’s most distinctive landscapes. From walking trails and water activities to skateboarding and family recreation, Bachman Lake Park offers something for visitors of all ages.

A Scenic Lake Destination in the Heart of Dallas

At the center of Bachman Lake Park is its namesake 205-acre city lake, which provides a peaceful setting for outdoor recreation while remaining connected to the energy of the surrounding city. Created from a reservoir built in 1903, the lake has been a defining feature of this Dallas landscape for more than a century.

The park’s 5K hike and bike trail, measuring approximately 3.08 miles, winds around the lake, offering a popular route for walkers, runners, and cyclists. The trail provides scenic views of the water and surrounding landscape, making it a favorite destination for those looking to enjoy fresh air and exercise without leaving the city. Picnic areas and a pavilion provide welcoming spaces for families, community gatherings, and outdoor celebrations.

The park’s landscape also reflects the area’s original riparian environment. Groves of native bald cypress grow along the water’s edge, creating shade and adding to the scenic character of the lake. The tree canopy is an important part of the park experience, although maintaining and expanding it has presented unique challenges because of the park’s location beneath the flight paths of Dallas Love Field.

Because of its proximity to the airport, the FAA and Dallas Parks Department have historically restricted the planting of tall tree species, including cedar, pecan, and cottonwood, near instrument landing systems. In some areas, trees that obstructed aviation infrastructure have even been required to be removed. These restrictions have made thoughtful tree planning especially important at Bachman Lake Park.

In March 2025, volunteers planted 38 new native Texas trees as part of the park’s ongoing revitalization efforts. The trees were approved by the City of Dallas Aviation Department and supported by the Texas Trees Foundation, helping rebuild canopy cover while meeting the park’s irrigation and aviation safety requirements.

One of the park’s most unique features is its location next to Dallas Love Field Airport. Aviation enthusiasts and visitors alike enjoy watching commercial aircraft take off and land overhead, making Bachman Lake one of the best spots in Dallas for plane spotting. The sight and sound of planes passing above the lake create a memorable experience that sets the park apart from other recreational destinations across the city.

Recreation, Aquatics, and Active Living

Bachman Lake Park is designed to support recreation for people of all ages and abilities. The park features playground spaces, open areas for outdoor activities, and connections to facilities that encourage active lifestyles.

The Bachman Recreation Center serves as an important community resource. Built in 1979, the center includes an indoor pool, fitness center, and gymnasium, providing opportunities for residents to stay active year-round through exercise, sports, swimming, and community programming.

During the warmer months, the park becomes a popular destination for families looking to cool off at the seasonal aquatic center. The facility provides a fun and welcoming environment where visitors can swim, play, and enjoy time together.

Whether visitors are taking a walk around the lake, enjoying a family picnic, participating in recreation programs, or spending a summer afternoon in the water, Bachman Lake Park continues to serve as a place where Dallas residents can connect and create memories.

A Hub for Skate Culture and Public Art

Bachman Lake Park is also home to one of Dallas’s premier skateboarding destinations: Bachman Skate Park. This 46,000-square-foot facility provides a dynamic space for skaters of all experience levels, from beginners learning new skills to experienced riders looking for challenging features.

The skate park includes a medium-sized flow bowl, a street plaza, a snake run, and a pool-style feature inspired by classic skateboarding designs. With open space to explore and a variety of terrain to navigate, the park has become a gathering place for Dallas’s skateboarding community.

Adding to the skate park’s identity is a vibrant public art mural created by artist Jieun Kim. Inspired by alebrijes, which are Mexican folk art sculptures of fantasy creatures, and the artist’s exploration of mythical creatures in Korean folk art, the mural features a mother dragon and her baby skating together. The artwork represents strength, tradition, and generational connection while blending cultural storytelling with skate culture.

The mural celebrates movement, heritage, and transformation, reflecting the diverse community that gathers at Bachman Lake Park.

A Place Where Dallas Comes Together

For decades, Bachman Lake Park has represented the many ways public spaces enrich city life. From its origins as a reservoir in the early 20th century to its evolution into a 205-acre public park, the landscape continues to adapt to the needs of the Dallas community.

Its combination of natural scenery, mature and newly planted trees, recreation, art, and community amenities makes it a unique destination within the Dallas park system. The park’s revitalization efforts demonstrate how thoughtful stewardship can balance environmental goals, community needs, and the practical requirements of its location beside an active airport.

From watching airplanes soar overhead and walking along the lake’s shoreline to skating, swimming, and enjoying time with family and friends, Bachman Lake Park offers countless ways to experience Dallas. As the city continues to grow, the park remains a place where people can gather, explore, and enjoy the shared experiences that make a community strong.
