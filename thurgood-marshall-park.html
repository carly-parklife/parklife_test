A Community Landmark Rooted in Service

For more than 60 years, Thurgood Marshall Park has served as a vibrant gathering place for families, athletes, and neighbors in southern Dallas. Founded in 1964 in the Red Bird community, the park’s 24.1 acres provide a welcoming destination where recreation, community programs, athletics, and outdoor spaces come together to enrich everyday life. From youth sports and fitness opportunities to family-friendly attractions and community celebrations, the park continues to reflect the values of inclusion, opportunity, and service.

Located in the Red Bird community, Thurgood Marshall Park has grown alongside the surrounding neighborhood, evolving into one of the area’s most active public spaces. Today, visitors enjoy a wide variety of amenities, including the Thurgood Marshall Recreation Center, athletic facilities, walking trails, playgrounds, picnic areas, football and baseball fields, and the popular Bahama Beach Family Water Park. Whether stopping by for a workout, attending a community event, cooling off with the family, or cheering from the sidelines during a football game, generations of Dallas residents have made lasting memories here.

From Red Bird Recreation Center to Thurgood Marshall Park

When the park was established in 1964, it served the growing Red Bird community as an important center for recreation and neighborhood activities. The Red Bird Recreation Center became a focal point for youth activities, educational programs, athletics, and community gatherings, helping strengthen connections among residents during a period of growth in southern Dallas.

The recreation center was formally dedicated in 1972 and continued serving the community for decades. In 1993, it was renamed the Thurgood Marshall Recreation Center in honor of Thurgood Marshall, the legendary civil rights attorney and the first Black Justice of the United States Supreme Court. The facility underwent a major renovation before reopening in May 2021, continuing its role as an important resource for recreation, education, and community programming.

Thurgood Marshall dedicated his career to advancing equality under the law, most notably through his successful arguments against school segregation before joining the nation’s highest court in 1967. Honoring Marshall through the recreation center and park reflects his lifelong commitment to expanding opportunity and creating pathways for future generations.

Recreation for Every Generation

Thurgood Marshall Park offers a diverse collection of recreational opportunities designed to serve residents of all ages. At the heart of the park is the Thurgood Marshall Recreation Center, which provides year-round programming for children, teens, adults, and seniors.

Following its renovation and reopening in May 2021, the recreation center continues to provide valuable indoor spaces for fitness, athletics, classes, educational programs, community gatherings, and recreational activities. Its fitness center, gymnasium, and multipurpose meeting rooms create opportunities for residents to stay active, learn new skills, and build lasting relationships with neighbors.

Outside, the park offers numerous amenities that encourage healthy living and outdoor recreation. Walking and jogging trails wind through the property, while playgrounds and picnic areas create inviting spaces for families to gather. Tennis courts, baseball fields, open green spaces, and athletic fields provide opportunities for both organized sports and casual recreation throughout the year.

Adjacent to the recreation center is Bahama Beach Family Water Park, one of Dallas’s early urban municipal water parks. Originally opened in May 2005, the seasonal attraction has become a popular summer destination for families, adding water slides, splash areas, and other family-friendly experiences to the recreational offerings at Thurgood Marshall Park.

A Home for Youth Sports and Community Leadership

Athletics have long played an important role at Thurgood Marshall Park, providing young people with opportunities to develop teamwork, discipline, confidence, and leadership both on and off the field.

The park’s athletic offerings include baseball fields, tennis courts, and the Earl “Coach” Thompson Football Field. The football field was rebuilt in 2025 with updated turf, LED lighting, new bleachers, and a scoreboard, creating a modern venue for local youth athletics. In 2026, the field was officially named in honor of Earl “Coach” Thompson, whose decades of service made a profound impact on young people throughout southern Dallas.

As the longtime leader of the Redbird Raiders Youth Outreach program, Coach Thompson devoted his life to mentoring young athletes while emphasizing education, character, and personal responsibility. His influence extended far beyond football, helping countless young people build confidence, overcome challenges, and pursue brighter futures.

Coach Thompson’s extraordinary commitment earned numerous honors throughout his lifetime. In 2017, he received the Presidential Lifetime Achievement Award, the highest recognition within the President’s Volunteer Service Award program, acknowledging decades of volunteer service to his community. In 2022, he was inducted into the Texas Black Sports Hall of Fame for his lasting contributions to youth sports and community leadership.

The naming of the football field ensures that future generations will continue to learn about a local leader whose example demonstrates the lasting impact one individual can have through dedication, compassion, and service.

Growing a New Canopy for the Future

The park’s landscape is also evolving as Dallas invests in restoring and strengthening its urban tree canopy. Reconstruction of the Earl “Coach” Thompson Football Field in 2025 required the removal of some older shade trees, creating an opportunity to reestablish the canopy with new native plantings.

Through the city’s Branching Out program in spring 2026, approximately 50 new trees were introduced throughout the park. Native North Texas species such as live oak, bur oak, cedar elm, and pecan are helping restore shade while supporting local ecology and creating a more resilient landscape for future generations.

These new plantings represent an investment not only in the park’s appearance but also in its long-term environmental health. As the trees mature, they will provide additional shade for visitors, enhance habitat, and contribute to a greener and more inviting community gathering space.

Continuing a Legacy of Opportunity

Today, Thurgood Marshall Park continues to embody the ideals represented by its namesake and the community leaders who have shaped its history. The park serves as a place where recreation meets education, where families gather, where young people develop skills and confidence, and where community history continues to be carried forward.

Whether visitors come to cool off at Bahama Beach, participate in recreation center programs, enjoy an afternoon picnic, explore the park’s outdoor spaces, or cheer on local athletes at the Earl “Coach” Thompson Football Field, Thurgood Marshall Park remains a welcoming destination that strengthens community connections.

More than six decades after its founding, the park continues to evolve while preserving its role as a place where opportunity, service, recreation, and neighborhood pride come together. With renewed athletic facilities, beloved community destinations, and a growing canopy of native trees, Thurgood Marshall Park is helping create a lasting legacy for generations of Dallas residents.
